Biryani is a globally celebrated, 
        aromatic South Asian mixed rice dish made with long-grain basmati rice
        layers of richly spiced meat (chicken, mutton, prawns,fish,panner,mushroom,etc), or vegetables.
    
    


    

            
        Chicken Biryani   

            

            
The secret to its signature taste lies in the dum cooking process, where half-cooked basmati rice is 
        layered over richly marinated chicken and slow-steamed to perfection.
